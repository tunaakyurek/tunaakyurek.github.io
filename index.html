Research internship · Aalto University, Dept. of Electrical Engineering & Automation

Real-time smartphone EKF

An iPhone streams its IMU, GNSS and magnetometer readings to a Raspberry Pi on a DJI RoboMaster S1, where an 8-state Kalman filter estimates pose, velocity and sensor biases in real time. I built the pipeline end to end and recorded 25 runs.

Independent project · simulation (Isaac Sim, ROS 2)

Quadruped exploration & return-home

A simulated Unitree Go2 explores a building it has never seen, localises five coloured flags as 3-D landmarks and walks back to its start. Frozen and tested on 40 held-out worlds, it returned home in 39.
