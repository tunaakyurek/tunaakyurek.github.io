Tuna Akyürek

Senior undergraduate student in Electrical and Electronics Engineering with a double major in Physics at Boğaziçi University.

My work focuses on state estimation, probabilistic mapping, closed-loop control, and robot perception, combining analytical modeling with experimental evaluation in simulation and on physical robot platforms.

Research areas: State estimation & Kalman filtering · Probabilistic occupancy mapping & exploration · Model-based & learning-based control · Monocular depth estimation & visual registration · Autonomous mobile & legged robotics

Senior design project (EE491–492) · Boğaziçi University

Actuated sonar-ring autonomy

A Kobuki mobile robot that autonomously explores an unknown maze, constructs a probabilistic occupancy map, and navigates to a goal using only an actuated ring of eight HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors. Research internship · Aalto University, Dept. of Electrical Engineering & Automation

Real-time smartphone EKF

An iPhone streams IMU, GNSS, and magnetometer measurements over UDP/TCP to a Raspberry Pi 4 onboard a DJI RoboMaster S1, where an 8-state Extended Kalman Filter estimates planar pose, forward speed, and inertial biases in real time across 25 experimental runs (641.8 s total).

Independent research · simulation (Isaac Sim, ROS 2)

Quadruped exploration & return-home

A simulated Unitree Go2 quadruped explores unseen indoor buildings, localizes five semantic target flags in 3-D, and returns to its starting pose. Following closed-loop failure diagnosis and locomotion stabilization, the closed system returned home in 30 of 30 unseen worlds where the inherited one managed 21.
