Work

Research projects, independent work and selected coursework. Each page starts with the problem, my part in it and the result; methods and sources are further down.

Selected projects

  • Senior design project (EE491–492) · Boğaziçi University · Sep 2025 – Jun 2026

    Actuated sonar-ring autonomy

    A Kobuki robot that explores an unknown maze, maps it and drives to a goal using only eight low-cost ultrasonic sensors on servo mounts.

  • Research internship · Aalto University, Dept. of Electrical Engineering & Automation · Jul – Sep 2025

    Real-time smartphone EKF

    An iPhone streams its IMU, GNSS and magnetometer readings to a Raspberry Pi on a DJI RoboMaster S1, where an 8-state Kalman filter estimates pose, velocity and sensor biases in real time.

  • Independent project · simulation (Isaac Sim, ROS 2) · Autumn 2026

    Quadruped exploration & return-home

    A simulated Unitree Go2 explores an unseen building, maps five coloured flags as 3-D landmarks and walks back to its start.

  • Course project · Boğaziçi University · Sep – Oct 2026

    Metric depth adaptation study

    Controlled metric adaptation of Depth-Anything-V2-Metric-Hypersim (Small and Base) across nine indoor benchmarks.

Other projects

  • Independent project · simulation (PyBullet, acados) · Summer 2026

    Quadrotor control benchmark

    A simulation benchmark that runs geometric SE(3) control, nonlinear MPC and learning-based controllers behind one interface and the same evaluation protocol.

  • Course project · Boğaziçi University · Sep – Oct 2026

    Robust classical visual front-end

    Target-only adaptive classical pre-processing for SIFT, RootSIFT, and ORB homography registration on HPatches.

Coursework

  • Course project · University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign · Fall 2024

    Reaction-wheel pendulum control

    Model, identify and control an underactuated pendulum — friction-compensated state feedback, an observer and energy-based swing-up — compared on the physical rig.

  • Coursework · Boğaziçi University · Fall 2025 – Jan 2026

    Robot control: arms and mobile robots

    A semester of ROS/C++ robot-control projects: inverse kinematics and Jacobian-level pick-and-place for serial arms in Gazebo, then planning and navigation for a TurtleBot, first in simulation and then on the lab robot.

  • Graduate coursework · Boğaziçi University · Spring 2026

    Multi-agent control reproductions

    Re-implementations, from the published equations, of a distributed formation–containment controller and an adaptive-consensus target tracker.
