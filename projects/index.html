Work

Research projects, independent engineering investigations, and selected technical coursework. Each study documents the problem formulation, system architecture, experimental protocol, quantitative outcomes, and scope limitations.

Selected projects

  • Senior design project (EE491–492) · Boğaziçi University · Sep 2025 – Jun 2026

    Actuated sonar-ring autonomy

    A Kobuki mobile robot that autonomously explores an unknown maze, constructs a probabilistic occupancy map, and navigates to a goal using only an actuated ring of eight HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors.

  • Research internship · Aalto University, Dept. of Electrical Engineering & Automation · Jul – Sep 2025

    Real-time smartphone EKF

    An iPhone streams IMU, GNSS, and magnetometer measurements over UDP/TCP to a Raspberry Pi 4 onboard a DJI RoboMaster S1, where an 8-state Extended Kalman Filter estimates planar pose, forward speed, and inertial biases in real time across 25 experimental runs (641.8 s total).

  • Independent research · simulation (Isaac Sim, ROS 2) · Autumn 2026

    Quadruped exploration & return-home

    A simulated Unitree Go2 quadruped explores unseen indoor buildings, localizes five semantic target flags in 3-D, and returns to its starting pose.

  • Course project · Boğaziçi University · Sep – Oct 2026

    Metric depth adaptation study

    Controlled metric adaptation of Depth-Anything-V2-Metric-Hypersim (Small and Base) across nine indoor benchmarks.

Other projects

  • Independent research · simulation (PyBullet, acados) · Summer 2026

    Quadrotor control benchmark

    A modular quadrotor control benchmark in PyBullet and acados that places geometric SE(3) control, nonlinear MPC, and reinforcement-learning policies behind a single state-to-wrench interface, wind-disturbance model, and real-time latency-profiling pipeline.

  • Course project · Boğaziçi University · Sep – Oct 2026

    Robust classical visual front-end

    Target-only adaptive classical pre-processing for SIFT, RootSIFT, and ORB homography registration on HPatches.

Coursework

  • Course project · University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign · Fall 2024

    Reaction-wheel pendulum control

    Electromechanical system identification, pole-placement state feedback, a 4-state Luenberger velocity observer, and energy-injection swing-up control implemented and compared on a physical reaction-wheel inverted pendulum.

  • Coursework · Boğaziçi University · Fall 2025 – Jan 2026

    Robot control: arms and mobile robots

    A five-module ROS and C++ robotics sequence spanning analytical and numerical inverse kinematics, Jacobian velocity-level pick-and-place among obstacles for 3-DOF manipulators in Gazebo, and A*, Pure Pursuit, and Dynamic Window autonomous exploration on a physical TurtleBot.

  • Graduate coursework · Boğaziçi University · Spring 2026

    Multi-agent control & consensus

    Independent MATLAB reproductions and diagnostic ablations of two decentralized multi-agent architectures: adaptive formation–containment control for heterogeneous linear swarms and cross-covariance-aware dynamic-cluster target tracking across a 1,000-node sensor network.
